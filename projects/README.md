# Projects

## Sovereign Harness

**Own your intelligence.** I’m building a personal AI operating system and second brain on Hermes. It lets you direct a team of specialist agents to research, plan and carry work through beyond the chat box. The shared OODA Harness gives people a common foundation they can build on together. Your sovereign sleeve holds the data, lived experience, prompts and ways of working that give you your edge. You control what you share. If a provider changes access or you want to try a different model, the aim is to take your context and ways of working with you.

The first problem I’m tackling is how long it takes to get useful work out of Hermes. The goal is a one-click install and a short path to putting your first team to work. I’m developing profile packs so you can choose the roles you need, from a Chief of Staff to someone who helps you work the hidden job market. Each pack pairs a reusable role with a private sleeve. Familiar characters and stories help you recognise what each role does. John Boyd’s OODA loop and Gregory Bateson’s work on how people make sense of their world inform how the roles orient and operate within other people’s decision cycles.

I’m testing paired roles where both agents keep a complete OODA. One takes the lead on the work while the other actively observes and challenges it. They can swap at a handoff. I’m developing evaluations that combine qualitative review with numbers tied to what each role is meant to achieve. I want to see where pairing improves the work enough to justify the extra coordination.

I’m preparing the shared harness for open-source release. You can [preview the five profile packs](https://sovereignharness.io/#profile-packs), [see the public Blake scaffold](https://github.com/benohanlon/ooda-role-blake) for an example of business development work, or [follow updates and alpha invitations](https://sovereignharness.io/#updates). I explore the argument for owning the means of cognition in [Survival of the Sovereign](../writing/README.md#survival-of-the-sovereign).
