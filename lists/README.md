# Reading and resources

Books and essays on ecosystem building, product, go-to-market, how people decide to take part, and open technology.

Start with P1 in the section you’re interested in. Use P2 when you have a more specific question.

## Ecosystems and strategy

### P1

- [Ecosystem Edge](https://ink.library.smu.edu.sg/lkcsb_research/6564/) · Arnoud De Meyer and Peter J. Williamson. Bring together complementary capabilities and work out the gives and gets that make participation worthwhile.
- [Platform Revolution](https://wwnorton.co.uk/books/9780393249132-platform-revolution) · Geoffrey G. Parker, Marshall W. Van Alstyne and Sangeet Paul Choudary. Design the interactions, incentives and rules of a platform.
- [The Interaction Field](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/erich-joachimsthaler/the-interaction-field/9781541730519/) · Erich Joachimsthaler. How repeated interactions among customers, partners and communities create shared value.
- [Wardley Maps](https://learnwardleymapping.com/book/) · Simon Wardley. Map what users need and the capabilities they depend on before deciding where to act.
- [Good Strategy/Bad Strategy](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/208668/good-strategy-bad-strategy-by-richard-rumelt/9780307886231/) · Richard Rumelt. Diagnose the challenge, set a guiding policy and choose coherent actions.
- [The Essence of Winning and Losing](https://slightlyeastofnew.com/wp-content/uploads/2010/03/essence_of_winning_losing.pdf) · John Boyd. Start with the original OODA diagram, then read [Boyd’s Real OODA Loop](https://slightlyeastofnew.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/boyds-real-ooda-loopx.pdf) by Chet Richards on orientation, feedback and implicit guidance.
- [Numbers Come Before Strategy](../writing/numbers-come-before-strategy.md) · Ben O’Hanlon. A small set of system-wide units of account gives teams and proposals an axis around which to orient, allowing for high alignment and loose coupling.

### P2

- [One from Many](https://www.deewhock.com/publications) · Dee Hock. How Visa’s independent members agreed what to govern together and what to decide locally.
- [Cynefin](https://cynefin.io/wiki/Cynefin) · Dave Snowden. Choose how to intervene when you can learn what works through small, safe-to-fail probes.
- [Vector Theory of Change](https://thecynefin.co/library/change-complexity-vector-theory-of-change/) · The Cynefin Company. Set a direction, try small interventions and use feedback to decide what to do next.
- [High Output Management](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/72467/high-output-management-by-andrew-s-grove-former-chairman-and-ceo-of-intel/) · Andrew S. Grove. Managerial leverage, team output and the routines that support both.
- [Turning the Flywheel](https://www.jimcollins.com/books/turningtheflywheel.html) · Jim Collins. Trace how each step reinforces the next and how the last strengthens the first.

## Product

### P1

- [Crossing the Chasm](https://www.wiley-vch.de/en/areas-interest/finance-economics-law/crossing-the-chasm-978-1-84112-063-8) · Geoffrey A. Moore. How adoption changes when you move from early adopters to mainstream buyers.
- [Leah’s Product-led Growth Guide 3.0](https://www.leahtharin.com/p/the-product-led-growth-guide) · Leah Tharin. How the product itself supports acquisition, activation and growth.

### P2

- [Just Enough Research](https://www.mulebooks.com/just-enough-research) · Erika Hall. Find out what users need before deciding what to build.
- [Why Community-Led Product Development Wins](https://creatoreconomy.so/p/community-led-product) · Peter Yang. Bring users into product development so they can help shape what gets built.
- [Shape Up](https://basecamp.com/shapeup) · Ryan Singer

## Developer relations and community

### P1

- [Developer Relations](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-1-4842-7164-3) · Caroline Lewko and James Parton. How to build and grow a developer programme.
- [The Business Value of Developer Relations](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-1-4842-3748-9) · Mary Thengvall. Connect developer relationships to outcomes the business cares about.
- [The Software Engineer’s Guidebook](https://www.engguidebook.com/) · Gergely Orosz. See the engineering side of the relationship, including how teams make trade-offs, ship and maintain software.
- [Buzzing Communities](https://www.feverbee.com/buzzingcommunities/) · Richard Millington. Build and sustain an active online community.

### P2

- [Developer Relations Activity Patterns](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/979-8-8688-1895-0) · Ted Neward, Scott T. McAllister, David Neal and Chris Woodruff. Choose activities for developer relations, developer experience and community management.
- [The Business of Belonging](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/the-business-of-belonging-how-to-make-community-your-competitive-advantage-p-9781119766117) · David Spinks. Connect what members value with why the organisation invests in community.
- [Patterns for Decentralised Organising](https://leanpub.com/patterns-for-decentralised-organising) · Richard D. Bartlett. Share the work of running a community, from welcoming members to maintaining its culture.

## Sales and GTM

### P1

- [The ultimate guide to value](https://alexmhsmith.substack.com/p/the-ultimate-guide-to-value) · Alex M H Smith. Ask what the other person gains and why it matters to them.
- [How to Master the Art of Selling](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/tom-hopkins/how-to-master-the-art-of-selling/9780446692748/) · Tom Hopkins. Discovery, qualification, handling objections and asking for a decision.
- [SHiFT!](https://shiftselling.com/book/) · Craig Elias and Tibor Shanto. Spot events that make a buyer more likely to act and use them to decide when to reach out.

**Copywriting**

- [Breakthrough Advertising Secrets](https://www.scientificadvertising.com/eugene_schwartz_live_at_rodale_press/) · Eugene Schwartz’s Rodale Press seminar. Start with the desire your audience already has and support the promise with proof.
- [The Boron Letters](https://www.thegaryhalbertletter.com/Products_%26_Affiliates/OriginalBoronLetters.html) · Gary Halbert. Study the market and mailing list, then write the offer in plain, direct language.

### P2

- [Never Split the Difference](https://www.blackswanltd.com/never-split-the-difference) · Chris Voss with Tahl Raz. Surface concerns, defend fees and clarify what has to happen for an agreement to go ahead. [Negotiation One-Sheet](https://info.blackswanltd.com/hubfs/Content/Negotiation-One-Sheet.pdf)
- [The Pyramid Principle](https://www.pearson.com/en-gb/subject-catalog/p/the-pyramid-principle/P200000015259/9781292763255) · Barbara Minto. Lead with the answer and organise the reasons and evidence underneath it.
- [How to ask for an introduction](https://vaughntan.org/fir) · Vaughn Tan. Ask whether someone is willing to help and give them a note they can forward.
- [Traction](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/289806/traction-by-mares-gabriel-weinberg-and-justin/9780241996935) · Gabriel Weinberg and Justin Mares. Choose and test acquisition channels before putting more resources behind one.
- [The Science of Scaling](https://www.stage2.capital/science-of-scaling) · Mark Roberge. Decide when repeatable customer success justifies scaling sales and how quickly to hire.
- [The Bowtie Standard](https://winningbydesign.com/resources/research/bowtie-standard/) · Jacco van der Kooij. Map recurring revenue from acquisition through onboarding, retention and expansion.
- [$100M Offers](https://www.acquisition.com/training/offers) · Alex Hormozi
- [Built to Grow](https://press.stripe.com/built-to-grow) · Stephanie Friedman

## AI, theory of mind and memetics

### P1

- [Steps to an Ecology of Mind](https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/chicago/S/bo3620295.html) · Gregory Bateson. Start with “Form, Substance and Difference” and ask what differences matter in the other person’s world.
- [The Society of Mind](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/Society-Of-Mind/Marvin-Minsky/9780671657130) · Marvin Minsky. How many simpler processes can work together to produce the capabilities we call mind.
- [Out of Control](https://kk.org/mt-files/outofcontrol/contents.php) · Kevin Kelly. Start with [“Hive Mind”](https://kk.org/mt-files/outofcontrol/ch2-a.html) for examples of how groups coordinate without a central controller.
- [When Does AI Augment Work?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12482) · Jiaying Wu, Caleb Ziems and colleagues. Assess whether AI improves the whole workflow, including human control, verification and recovery.
- [We Are Hosts for Memes](https://wearehostsformemes.com/). Take the meme’s-eye view of how ideas, stories and habits spread.
- [Antimemetics](https://nayafia.spicytakes.org/post/2025-03-26-introducing-antimemetics-my-new-book) · Nadia Asparouhova. Why some ideas resist spreading or circulate in private.

### P2

- [Supersizing the Mind](https://academic.oup.com/book/6746) · Andy Clark. How tools and environments can become part of thinking.
- [Ironies of Automation](https://tc.ifac-control.org/4/1/newsletter/ironies-of-automation/@@download/file/Bainbridge1983_Automatica_Ironies%20of%20automation.pdf) · Lisanne Bainbridge. Why automation can leave people with the hardest monitoring and recovery work.
- [Spiral Dynamics](https://www.spiraldynamics.com/book/book.htm) · Don Beck and Christopher Cowan. A lens for examining how people’s values relate to their circumstances.
- [The Ellipsis Manual](https://nci.university/resources/) · Chase Hughes. Read for observation, listening and rapport. Check what you infer against what the person says.
- [What Is Structural Memetics?](https://empathy.guru/2019/04/06/what-is-structural-memetics-and-why-does-it-matter/). How relationships within a group shape what it can think and produce.
- [Cultural Evolution and Memetics](https://pespmc1.vub.ac.be/Papers/Memetics-Springer.pdf) · Francis Heylighen and Klaas Chielens. A research reference on how ideas are taken up, remembered, expressed and passed on.

## Open technology and sovereignty

### P1

- [The Cathedral and the Bazaar](https://nakamotoinstitute.org/library/the-cathedral-and-the-bazaar/) · Eric S. Raymond. How users become contributors and build software together in the open.
- [The Internet of Money](https://aantonop.com/the-internet-of-money/) · Andreas M. Antonopoulos. What changes when money can move over an open network that anyone can use.
- [The Sovereign Individual](https://www.simonandschuster.co.uk/books/The-Sovereign-Individual/James-Dale-Davidson/9781439144732) · James Dale Davidson and William Rees-Mogg. How information technology changes the balance of power between individuals and states.
- [Survival of the Sovereign](../writing/README.md) · Ben O’Hanlon. How coordination technologies can be captured at civilisational scale, and why open technology will win out in the intelligence counter-revolution.

### P2

- [From Territorial to Monetary Sovereignty](https://scholarship.law.columbia.edu/faculty_scholarship/2279/) · Katharina Pistor. Who holds monetary power when private institutions issue money and states backstop the system?
- [Peer to Peer: The Commons Manifesto](https://www.fulcrum.org/concern/monographs/2v23vx522?locale=en) · Michel Bauwens, Vasilis Kostakis and Alex Pazaitis
- [Grassroots Economics: Reflection and Practice](https://grassrootseconomics.org/book/) · William O. Ruddick
