# EOS packages

A package combines services to achieve an outcome a team values. The mix depends on what the team can already do, what others can contribute and what each party stands to gain. [Capabilities and services](capabilities.md)

## Early adoption through trusted partners

**Case from practice.** RealFi needed its first users to try a new stablecoin on Cardano. The accelerator worked with stake pool operators who already had relationships with the people RealFi wanted to reach. It combined ecosystem intelligence and insights, network making and relationship building, and onboarding. RealFi used on-chain activity alongside online influence to identify partners. Operators brought trusted routes to their delegators and earned points tied to what those delegators earned. [RealFi case](../cases/README.md#getting-early-adoption-through-a-network-people-already-trust)

## Engagement and retention

**Working offer.** Managed Engagement helps teams keep an existing community informed and connected while they build. It combines ecosystem communications, community engagement, facilitation and ecosystem intelligence. The team provides product updates, access to the people building it and someone to act on feedback. The Collective can organise newsletters and office hours, handle follow-up and bring useful feedback back to the team. Scope, cadence and terms are agreed for each engagement.

## Delivery models

- **Centralised.** A shared ecosystem team leads the work.
- **Federated.** Teams or partners deliver the work together.
- **Distributed.** A team delivers the work independently.

A supported package can become a self-serve product once the approach is repeatable and teams can use the guidance and tools independently.
