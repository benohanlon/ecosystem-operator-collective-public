<a name="§a-harvest-of-the-mind-feast-or-famine"></a>

## <strong>A harvest of the mind. Feast or famine?</strong>

AI promises us a utopia. A future of unparalleled abundance, where robotics and automation handle the mundane, leaving humans to pursue connection and purpose. Automation that frees humanity from labour. The end of the age-old bargain of wage labour that requires human beings to sell their time in order to exist. A future that frees us from the shackles of necessity, to face humanity’s true frontier: the golden creative leisure. And, perhaps it will.

> <em>The coming era of joblessness is not a crisis, not a threat but a blessing - an initiation through which humanity is released from compelling necessity and invited to remember its genius, creative nature - a quiet, graceful advent that breaks the ancient spell binding life to labor. - [Elio D'Anna](<https://open.substack.com/users/29457580-elio-danna?utm_source=mentions>)</em>

But a post-scarcity society fundamentally requires abundance to be accessible, not just producible. If abundance becomes cheap to produce, but the tools that produce it are owned and gated, you lose the ability to produce for yourself. Humanity doesn’t get a utopia. It gets dependency and technological unemployment. Intensified surveillance capitalism. Diminished economic autonomy. And famine.

Because abundance denied leads to poverty and famine. You may picture famine as an empty land, a drought, a crop that didn’t come. Sometimes it is. But often the food exists, and a wall of price, or wage, or law, or politics stands between the hungry and the harvest. In the Irish famine, food left the country while the people who grew it starved, because they did not own the land it grew on. Famine isn’t always a shortage of food. It is often about who is allowed to eat.

And a harvest of the mind, of the intellectual commons, is what is happening. Humanity’s collective knowledge, unpaid labour, and the collated output of thought across generations. The intellectual inheritance of our children, used to train AI without asking, without paying, and sold back to us. Now it threatens to automate away meaningful work while stripping the public of the upside, building derivative systems on intellectual property that was stolen.

> <em>"We see a future where intelligence is a utility, like electricity or water, and people buy it from us on a meter." - Sam Altman, BlackRock US Infrastructure Summit, March 2026</em>

[Watch on YouTube](<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wc3OSJ2_6CA&t=346s>)

The purpose of a system is what it does. If it radically reorganises the economics of society with little regard for long-held rights such as intellectual property or the inevitable legal disputes, then that is its purpose. If it concentrates wealth from humanity's accumulated output into fewer and fewer hands, then that too is its purpose. And, if it harvests humanity's inheritance, sells it back to us with a promise to make us more efficient, then automates away the work it learned from us, then that is its purpose. Elite labs in an arms race, harvesting at a scale and speed Napster could not have imagined in its wildest fever dreams. And Napster was sued out of existence for a fraction of it. So was LimeWire, litigated to death after it.

![](<../assets/dear-anon-are-you-joining-the-permanent/02.png>)

You don’t own the rails. The United States has issued an export control directive suspending access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5 for foreign nationals, including foreign-national staff inside the labs. Read that again. Your access to the most powerful tools ever built can be switched off because of where you were born.

The interface is not sovereign. It is permissioned.

<a name="§the-old-world-is-dying-the-new-world-struggles-to-be-born"></a>

## <strong>The old world is dying. The new world struggles to be born</strong>

Technology, not ideology, determines the structure of power. Money is a coordination technology, arguably the oldest one, and it has been captured before, used for civilisational control and subversion, to reorganise society. Whoever owns the infrastructure owns you. Sovereignty, the individual’s and the nation’s, is what a captured system takes.

In the 20th Century, the global money supply was captured, and war was waged through credit. We looked at that in my last post.

[MONEY IS A COORDINATION TECHNOLOGY THAT HAS BEEN CAPTURED.](<https://ecosystemoperator.substack.com/p/money-is-a-coordination-technology>)

[![MONEY IS A COORDINATION TECHNOLOGY THAT HAS BEEN CAPTURED.](<../assets/dear-anon-are-you-joining-the-permanent/03.png>)](<https://ecosystemoperator.substack.com/p/money-is-a-coordination-technology>)

Consider the tools. Credit, the monetary supply, and the IMF. An economic system captured before you were born, through fiat. A world order pegged to the dollar, run on currency debasement, funding endless wars and propaganda.

> <em>“the very operation of the money system is anathema to democratic self-governance”  Katharina Pistor, From Territorial to Monetary Sovereignty, 2017</em>

The legitimacy of that world order, the rules-based international order, has drained away, yet its institutions grind on, dysfunctional and decaying. The burgeoning administrative state, US dollar hegemony, globalisation, the liberal democratic order, all unravelling. A long, slow, managed decline. And do not imagine some statesman will step in to set it right, Anon. The apparatus is too large for that now. Most politicians are useless, and the handful of decent ones are pulling levers that connect to nothing, because the system is captured and ungovernable from the ballot box. Money was captured first, then used to capture other systems like governance, media, markets.

> <em>“The old world is dying, and the new world struggles to be born; now is the time of monsters.” - Antonio Gramsci</em>

The money supply was never on the ballot. Neither will the means of cognition.

Behold, a white horse. The four horsemen of this century, as Charles Hoskinson calls them, are on the horizon. Trailing fear, uncertainty and doubt. AI extends cognition without consciousness. Quantum extends computation. Synthetic biology transforms living cells. Nanotech turns matter into a machine from the atom up.

[Watch on YouTube](<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uragYmFhNIk&t=1501s>)

Who will seize these tools? Tools that can extend humanity’s capabilities and restrict them are systems of control as well as abundance. Without a mechanism to govern them that is neutral and transparent, they will inevitably fall under the control of either concentrated capital or the deep state. Whoever controls the tools that force radical reorganisation of economics and power structures controls what the world reorganises into.

In the 21st Century, war will be waged through cognition. AI is a coordination technology too. Arguably a base layer, below money. If you don’t control the infrastructure and supply you will suffer both floods and droughts, made dependent on a system controlled by those who do. Nations too. You will need cognitive capital to compete. Abundant today, and the day it is rationed, or switched off, you cannot. It is a rare person who buys a house without a mortgage. It will be a rarer one who gets ahead without these tools. Soon, impossible.

<a name="§cui-bono"></a>

### <strong>Cui bono?</strong>

People fear becoming part of a permanent underclass because historically, the cost of systemic economic shifts has been overwhelmingly borne by the working and middle classes. So that fear is not irrational.

Technological revolutions have caused mass displacement, dispossession, and disenfranchisement. They have stripped people of their livelihoods, long-held rights and way of life. Created a massive, desperate labour pool with no choice but to migrate. These profound upheavals created the modern world.

So the question is simple. Cui bono? Who benefits? Who owns the upside? Who bears the cost? What does life mean to you and your loved ones if you can’t work, must rent, and only have superficial choices to mask a complete lack of control over the system itself?

Everything is at stake. Everything you know, everyone you love, all you have lived for. Every soul who ever lived is implicated, because every trace of human life and memory is becoming substrate for AI, the intellectual commons harvested like spice, and enclosed. Every person born is a participant whether they choose it or not. This is a total war for cognitive supremacy, sovereignty and ownership. A battle for the brain and its accumulated output across history itself.

![](<../assets/dear-anon-are-you-joining-the-permanent/04.jpeg>)

You feel sure the authors of this upheaval will be obliged to answer for it. But power does not answer willingly to those who suffer under it. It never has. The elite keep the privileges and abandon the obligations, and call it progress.

Nobody is coming to save us, Anon. We will have to decide our own fate.

Are you joining the permanent underclass?
