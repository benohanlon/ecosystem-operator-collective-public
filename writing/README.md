## Numbers come before strategy

To scale a team or an ecosystem, agree what winning means before choosing a strategy. A small set of visible, system-wide units of account gives teams a shared axis to orient around. You can judge proposals by how they would move those numbers. That’s how you get high alignment with loose coupling. Teams can choose their own strategies and experiment in parallel. In a decentralised ecosystem, the goal is capital productivity, measured by how much durable economic value each unit of capital produces. The growth flywheel shows how each team’s work reinforces the rest, and the numbers show whether growth is compounding.

## Survival of the Sovereign

Why open technology will win out in the intelligence counter-revolution, an information insurgency already underway.

Money shows how a coordination technology can be captured at civilisational scale. AI reaches deeper, into the means of cognition. Act I asks who gets to share in the abundance AI promises when capabilities built from our intellectual inheritance are enclosed and rented back to us. Act II looks back at print, steam and enclosure to show technology as an agent of change, a conduit for coordination and an ecology that shapes incentives and behaviour. Print carried a new language of rights across distributed networks, turning scattered grievances into organised political force.

In *The Event Horizon*, fear of joining a permanent underclass adds to the latent narrative energy in the cognitive superposition, the field of interacting minds, models and stories. Open alternatives give people a way to act on that pressure. Sovereignty is the selection pressure. Individuals, businesses and states have reasons to build, fund and adopt systems they can run and adapt themselves. Independent teams already build on each other’s open models, harnesses and tools. Each contribution makes the next one easier. As people move their workloads to alternatives that offer lower costs, more control and greater resilience, they put pressure on the fees and margins of closed labs.
